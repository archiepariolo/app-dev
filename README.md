# One Piece

**One Piece** is my favorite series. It's a *shonen* manga by **Eiichiro Oda** that started in 1997, and it's also an anime.

---

## Premise

Monkey D. Luffy ate the Gum-Gum Fruit as a kid, which turned his body into rubber. He sets out to find the legendary treasure **One Piece** and become the *King of the Pirates*.

> "I'm gonna be King of the Pirates!"
> — Monkey D. Luffy

## The Straw Hat Pirates

1. Monkey D. Luffy – Captain
2. Roronoa Zoro – Swordsman
3. Nami – Navigator
4. Usopp – Sniper
5. Sanji – Cook
6. Tony Tony Chopper – Doctor
7. Nico Robin – Archaeologist
8. Franky – Shipwright
9. Brook – Musician
10. Jinbe – Helmsman

## Why I Like It

- Great world-building
  - Many islands, each with its own culture
  - Mysteries that pay off after years
- Memorable characters and villains
- Themes of **friendship**, **freedom**, and *following your dream*

## Bounty Code Example

Luffy's bounty can be written as a variable:

```python
luffy_bounty = 3_000_000_000
print(f"Luffy's bounty is {luffy_bounty} berries")
```

Inline code works too: use `print()` to show output.
